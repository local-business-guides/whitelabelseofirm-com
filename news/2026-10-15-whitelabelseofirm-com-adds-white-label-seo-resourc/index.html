Practical points for agencies

White Label SEO involves having another provider carry out SEO work that an agency offers under its own brand. For an agency considering this arrangement, practical questions include what work is included, how responsibilities are divided and how progress will be communicated.

A clear scope helps an agency understand what it's buying and what it can explain to customers. Reporting also deserves attention. Agencies should consider how they'll review completed work, handle questions and keep customer expectations consistent with the agreed services.

These considerations are useful when comparing providers or reviewing an existing arrangement. Having written, video and audio resources available this week gives agency owners and teams a choice of formats as they consider those decisions.
